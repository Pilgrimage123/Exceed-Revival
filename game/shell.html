UX & Accessibility

A card that hits goes to your Gauge, ready to fuel Ultras and Exceed.

A card that misses goes to your discard pile instead — gone until you draw or Reshuffle it back.

Force (the cost of Moving or Boosting) is paid by throwing cards away, too — your hand, Gauge, position, and Life are all one resource loop.

How the CPU works

All three tiers share the same underlying decision-maker (Monte Carlo strike selection, lethal detection, life-aware Exceed timing, evaluated Boosts). The only difference between tiers is how often that decision gets thrown away in favor of a random legal one instead: 40% of the time for Student, 20% for Warrior, never for Champion.
