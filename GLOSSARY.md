# Exceed Revival

Digital implementation and AI engine for the Exceed Fighting System card game.

## Language

**Arena Space**:
One of the nine linear positions (`0` through `8` internally, displayed as `1` through `9`) occupied by fighters and board tokens.
_Avoid_: Tile, square, slot

**Range**:
The relative distance (`1` through `8`) between two fighters on the arena, distinct from **Arena Space** (`0` through `8`).
_Avoid_: Distance, spaces away

**Card Key**:
The canonical template identifier of a card (e.g., `sweep`, `hadoken`) shared by all physical copies of that card, used across the **Information Set** and **Modern Native** boundary instead of runtime instance UIDs.
_Avoid_: Card ID, UID

**Card DSL**:
The data-only declarative representation of fighter rosters, card stats, and mechanic primitives derived from the checked-in card catalog.
_Avoid_: Card schema, roster JSON

**Classic JS**:
The legacy JavaScript CPU planner kept temporarily for unverified fighters, modal prompts, and strength benchmarking until retired by the Differential Promotion Gate.
_Avoid_: Legacy AI, old CPU

**Modern Native**:
The compiled Rust/WASM CPU planner implementing belief modeling, game-theoretic Strike resolution, and information-set neutral search.
_Avoid_: New AI, Rust bot

**Differential Promotion Gate**:
The release bar requiring zero rules, legality, or belief-state mismatches against the JS rules oracle across all shipped fighters plus verified play strength before Modern Native replaces Classic JS.
_Avoid_: Action parity check, parity gate

**Effect IR**:
The typed, deterministic set of declarative mechanic opcodes representing card and character abilities without arbitrary script callbacks.
_Avoid_: Card hooks, effect scripts

**DSL Parity**:
The per-fighter verification status (`verified` or `partial`) indicating whether a fighter's Effect IR passes the Differential Promotion Gate against the JS rules oracle.
_Avoid_: Fighter readiness, port status

**Information Set**:
The complete set of public game state plus a player's own private hand and known deck order, excluding all hidden opponent cards.
_Avoid_: Game state, private state

**Enemy CPU**:
The opponent seat controlled by the AI at the selected difficulty tier.
_Avoid_: Opponent AI, bot

**Trainer**:
The human-perspective advisory engine that evaluates positions at full-strength GTO strictly from the player's Information Set.
_Avoid_: Hint engine, advisor

**Beginner Coach**:
The plain-language onboarding presentation layer offering on-demand move tips and contextual mode guidance without raw search metrics.
_Avoid_: Onboarding tips, tutorial mode

**Study Mode**:
The opt-in glass-box presentation layer exposing search lines, Nash mixes, opponent hand-threat probabilities, and post-strike EV review.
_Avoid_: Debug overlay, pro HUD

**Strike Preview**:
A pure, fog-of-war-safe projection of a candidate attack's post-`Before` Arena Space, Range coverage, projected damage, and lethal status derived solely from the active player's `Information Set`.
_Avoid_: Attack sim, hover calc

**Public-State Lethal**:
A pre-reveal indicator that a playable attack deals damage greater than or equal to the opponent's remaining life against currently visible board modifiers, before hidden opponent responses are known.
_Avoid_: Guaranteed kill, sure lethal

**Confirmed Lethal**:
A post-reveal KO state verified by deterministic Strike resolution after both attacks are face-up.
_Avoid_: Hover lethal, predicted KO

**Trigger Timeline**:
The ordered two-pass sequence of Strike resolution phases (`Reveal & Speed`, `Faster: Before -> Range -> Hit & Damage -> After`, `Slower: Before -> Range -> Hit & Damage -> After or STUNNED`, `Cleanup`).
_Avoid_: Combat log, beat list

**Force**:
The spendable resource generated by discarding cards from Hand or Gauge to pay for Move, Boosts, Change Cards, and Force-cost effects; Normals and Specials generate 1 Force, and Ultras generate 2 Force.
_Avoid_: Mana, energy, discard cost

**Force Overpayment**:
Paying a Force cost with cards whose total Force exceeds the requirement (such as pitching a 2-Force Ultra for a 1-Force cost), with any excess Force lost.
_Avoid_: Exact Force only, overcharge

**Gauge**:
The face-up resource zone populated primarily by attacks that connect during Cleanup, spent to pay for Ultras, Exceeding, Criticals, and Cancels, or discarded as Force.
_Avoid_: Meter, super bar

**Ultra**:
A high-impact signature card whose Strike side costs Gauge to reveal and counts as 2 Force when discarded from Hand or Gauge to pay a Force cost.
_Avoid_: Super, finisher

**Advantage**:
A Strike-granted priority state giving the holder the next turn regardless of who initiated; if multiple Advantage grants occur in one Strike, the most recent grant wins.
_Avoid_: Priority, extra turn

**Stun**:
The interruption state triggered immediately when a faster attack deals damage strictly greater than the defender's effective Guard, discarding the slower card before it can activate.
_Avoid_: Guard break, cancel

**Reshuffle**:
Returning the discard pile to form a new deck, allowed once per game (either manually or automatically on an empty-deck draw/Wild Swing); needing a second reshuffle loses the game immediately.
_Avoid_: Deck-out, mill loss

**Turn**:
A single player's active decision window (1-indexed), spanning any chained Cancels or Extra Actions until turn control passes or a Strike concludes, distinct from individual log action entries.
_Avoid_: Round, step

**Match Event**:
A typed, engine-emitted record of a state transition during play that feeds the combat log, visual effects, Strike breakdown, and UI/audio observers, distinct from **Trigger Timeline** snapshots.
_Avoid_: FX item, log entry

**Interaction Mode**:
The single active human decision state currently awaiting input from the player, paired with its contextual help copy.
_Avoid_: UI mode, modal flag
